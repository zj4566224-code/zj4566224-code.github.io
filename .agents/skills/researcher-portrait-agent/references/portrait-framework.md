# Portrait framework

Write `author_portrait.md` in Chinese unless the user requests another language.

## Required structure

```markdown
# <Scholar> 学术研究画像

## 语料与方法说明
State included papers, date range, exclusions, version handling, and evidence limits.

## 研究阶段分析
### 阶段一：<plain-language label> (<years>)
Explain the driving question, dominant methods, representative paper IDs, intellectual character, and the evidence for transition. Repeat only as many stages as the corpus supports.

## 作者研究画像
Write 6-10 connected paragraphs.

## 局限与待确认事项
List corpus gaps, low-confidence metadata, and interpretive uncertainties.
```

## Synthesis requirements

Weave together five threads: the unifying question, evolution of the research program, change in how the field sees the subject, the researcher's recurring problem-solving fingerprint, and evidence-based future directions.

The stage analysis may name papers and IDs. The flowing portrait should synthesize at idea level instead of reciting one finding after another. Avoid unsupported psychological claims about the author. Describe observable research choices, such as repeated data sources, model strategies, comparison baselines, or movement between levels of analysis.

Differentiate three evidence levels:

- `Documented`: directly supported by cards.
- `Cross-paper synthesis`: a pattern supported by multiple named paper IDs.
- `Interpretation`: a plausible reading that must be labeled as such.

Do not claim a stage transition was caused by an event, dataset, or earlier result unless the papers or reliable metadata say so. Use "the corpus shifts toward" when cause is unknown.

## General-reader standard

Use plain business Chinese. Define unavoidable technical terms on first use. Prefer concrete descriptions over academic praise. Avoid words such as "groundbreaking" or "important" unless reporting a sourced assessment.
